SITEMAP CRAWLER · LIVE GOOGLE SHEET · DAILY RECHECK · SEARCH CONSOLE + GA4

AUDITFORGE

The audit that clocks in every morning.

01 · the problem The SEO sends the audit, then everyone goes quiet. Three weeks later I'm asking "what's actually been fixed?" and getting a shrug. The audit was the deliverable. The fix never was.

02 · what I shipped Give it a sitemap. It crawls every URL and everything they point to into a live Google Sheet: 118,008 pages, one laptop, one night. Every row has a proof link that scrolls to the exact text, and the correction next to it: the redirect, the heading it should be, page by page.

03 · the follow-through Every morning at 6 it rechecks every finding on the live site and emails me: done, or still open. Delete a row from the sheet and that finding stops being chased. The team's edits are the spec. No ticket to me.

04 · did it matter? Search Console and GA4 wired in: every fixed page, before vs after, against pages not fixed yet. A control group, not a dashboard. At 14, 30 and 90 days an impact report emails itself. Which fixes paid off. Which were theatre.

02
LLM WRITER · FACT GUARD · APPROVAL GATE · COST CAP · SMARTLEAD REPORTING

CADENCE

Four emails per prospect. Zero that a machine let through unchecked.

01 · the problem Sales researched every prospect by hand and wrote four emails each. An hour per lead, quality off a cliff after lunch, and the occasional congratulations on a funding round that belonged to someone else.

02 · what I shipped A sheet of company names in, a finished four-touch cadence out. It finds a real news trigger from this quarter, frames the decision the prospect is actually facing, and writes to that. 7,200 prospects in the last run.

03 · the gate Every email passes a rules check, a fact guard that ignores our own framing and flags only real claims, a sampled second judge, and an approval gate, the only thing allowed to mark a row Ready. A bad sentence gets rebuilt and re-checked. A wrong company gets held, never repaired.

04 · the call · the scoreboard Hard cap: $0.90 per 1,000 prospects. Near the cap, the expensive steps switch off in order; the fact guard never does. Reporting is a Smartlead scoreboard that pulls sent, opens and replies at 7am and redeploys itself. No server, no database. Sales takes the credit. I have made my peace with that.

03
VERIFIED AI OUTPUT · 66 CHECKS · SHEETS API · NEXT.JS

REDLINE

The QA pass that is not allowed to make things up.

01 · the problem A formatting miss went live after four people read the page and all four read past it. Casing, a double space, a growth rate that did not survive arithmetic. Invisible in a CMS field, embarrassing on a published report.

02 · what I shipped Paste a live URL. It runs 66 checklist rows over the parsed page, requests every link and image, recomputes the CAGR instead of reading it, compares against the last four published reports, and writes a Yes or No row to the master sheet.

03 · the call Three rows genuinely need a model; spelling and tone are not regex problems. So the model is never trusted, only verified: it must quote the exact span, the server confirms that span exists character for character, and the fix must be a minimal edit of the quote.

04 · the proof An early build quoted a real sentence and invented a typo inside it. Now anything failing a guard is discarded, counted, and shown in the run summary. Drift is visible instead of silent. No API key? Those rows read "Not run", not a guess. Live and in use.

LIVE URL→ 66 RULES→ AI, THEN VERIFIED→ YES / NO TO SHEET ✓

04
SOURCE-LEVEL TEARDOWN · 13 WEIGHTED FACTORS · SEARCH CONSOLE OAUTH · HREFLANG REPORT

X-RAY

Turns "why are we losing?" into a ranked to-do list.

01 · the problem "Why does that page outrank ours?" was being answered with instinct and the phrase "content is king", which is not a roadmap.

02 · what I shipped Point it at a URL. It finds the page each of four rival research firms ranks with for the same intent, tears both down at source level, and scores the gap across 13 factors: depth, schema, in-content links, E-E-A-T, citations, AI-Overview coverage, down to image alt text.

03 · the call Every factor carries an explicit weight, and that is the whole product. A weight can be argued with in a meeting and tuned when it's wrong; a gut feeling can only be agreed with or ignored. Body links are counted apart from the menu, because 400 nav links is not internal linking.

04 · the backlog Not a diagnosis, a to-do list, biggest gap first. Search Console OAuth adds live rank, CTR and clicks to every row; bulk mode runs a whole CSV; a weekly hreflang report makes "is Japan growing?" a filter, not a project. Rivals that 403 the crawler get marked blocked, not failed.

05
NEXT.JS · SEARCH CONSOLE + GA4 · URL INSPECTION API · NO DATABASE

PULSE

Every property, one screen, refreshed before anyone asks.

01 · the problem "How are we doing?" was answered with six browser tabs, a screenshot, and a number already three days old by the time it hit Slack.

02 · what I shipped One read-only screen for every Search Console and GA4 property the account can see: clicks, impressions, CTR, position with the change vs last period; pages in positions 1–10, 11–20, 21–30; pages that dropped five places or vanished; queries one nudge from page one; users on the site right now.

03 · the call Google does not expose the indexing report through its API, so I rebuilt it. Sitemap URLs plus every page with impressions count as indexed for free; the rest go through the URL Inspection quota, 2,000 a day, resumable, cached in the browser. Slow, honest, and the first time anyone here could see the not-indexed list.

04 · the detail that bit me Search Console lags three days, so every range ends three days ago, or every Monday looks like a traffic crash. Tokens stay server-side, sign-in is an allowlist, and it refreshes every five minutes so "any update?" gets answered with a URL.
